Manifesto — 01

We build rooms, not pages.

Timberhall is a fictional digital studio working at the seam of software, space and story. We treat interfaces as places you enter — warm, tactile, a little strange — and we ship them fast, quietly, and to spec. Everything here is procedural: the wood, the light, the noise on the glass.
